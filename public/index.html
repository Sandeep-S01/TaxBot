FAQ

Questions before you try TaxBot?

Keep the first step simple: send a message on WhatsApp, then review the books before using them for reporting.

Does TaxBot replace my CA? add

No. TaxBot assists with capture, extraction, summaries, and exports. CA review is recommended before filing or statutory submission.

What GST flows are supported in v1? add

V1 focuses on regular GST: B2B/B2C, CGST/SGST/IGST splits where state codes are available, exempt/nil-rated entries, and basic ITC from purchases and expenses.

What happens when AI is unsure? add

Low-confidence or duplicate-looking entries are marked as review-needed so the ledger does not silently treat uncertain data as clean.
